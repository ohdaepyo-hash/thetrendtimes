## First comment by the submitter

> I wanted to see whether what SaaS companies report about AI lines up with how AI is priced on public pages. Sources:
>
> - Gupta, "From Tokens to Credits to Outcomes" (https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30701v2): 75 coded rows, pages captured 2026-09-16 to 09-17. 16 of 29 observable AI-native rows (55%) are subscription with usage quota; 23 of 52 publish no price; outcome pricing appears in 1 of 52 AI-native rows.
> - monday.com Q3 2026 (https://ir.monday.com/news-and-events/news-releases/news-details/2026/monday-com-Announces-Third-Quarter-2026-Results-new/default.aspx): $377.5M revenue, +19%, NRR 108%, $36M AI ARR.
> - Klaviyo Q3 2026 (https://investors.klaviyo.com/newsroom/news/news-details/2026/Klaviyo-Delivers-Solid-Q3-with-Enterprise-Growth-Multi-Product-Strength-and-Agent-Traction/default.aspx): $387.2M, +25%, NRR 108%, no separate AI revenue figure.
> - Freshworks Q3 2026 (https://ir.freshworks.com/news/news-details/2026/Freshworks-Reports-Record-Third-Quarter-2026-Results/default.aspx): $245.4M, +14%, NRR 104%, no AI revenue figure.
> - Kaltura Q3 2026 (https://investors.kaltura.com/news-releases/news-release-details/kaltura-announces-financial-results-third-quarter-2026): $47.3M, +8%, NRR 97%, 15 AI deals worth $3.8M combined TCV.
>
> Caveats: the earnings figures are company-reported and non-GAAP definitions differ, four vendors are not a sample, and the audit reads pricing pages, not contracts, with one primary coder. If you price an AI feature, did you meter it separately from the seat?
