### 2/7

Sentiment first. Of 14,428 respondents, 31.6% are very favorable toward AI tools at work and 30.4% somewhat favorable. That's the 62% headline.

2025 was 60%, after 70%+ in 2023 and 2024.

By experience: 53% favorable at 1-5 years, 69% at 16+ years.

### 3/7

The survey says 87% had "some level of trust", up from 31% last year. Read that with care.

2025 asked how much developers trust AI accuracy (33% did, 46% didn't). 2026 asks when they trust it. The 87% adds up four conditional answers. The question changed.
