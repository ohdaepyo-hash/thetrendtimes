## First comment by the submitter

> The post is a reading of five primary sources, each with its own sample:
>
> - Sonar (https://www.sonarsource.com/blog/state-of-code-developer-survey-report-2027/): 1,026 developers and engineering managers. AI generates or heavily assists 65% of committed code (42% last year), 76% say they generate AI code faster than they can verify it, and "always check before committing" fell from 48% to 39%.
> - JetBrains (https://blog.jetbrains.com/research/2026/08/how-much-code-do-developers-really-let-agents-write/): 15,000+ professional developers. About 47% fully agent-generated, 38% AI-assisted, 27% manual, from range midpoints.
> - JetBrains (https://blog.jetbrains.com/research/2026/11/state-of-developer-ecosystem-2026/): 51% of tech managers say their org tracks token usage, 30% rank it among the most insightful metrics.
> - GitKraken (https://www.gitkraken.com/reports/state-of-ai): 554 respondents from its own community. 84% feel more productive, 20% can measure it.
> - DORA (https://dora.dev/insights/finding-balance-in-the-era-of-tokenmaxxing/): commentary on token leaderboards and outcome metrics.
>
> Caveats: everything is self-reported, Sonar and GitKraken sell tools for the problems they describe, and Sonar's 65% (generated or heavily assisted) is not the same measure as JetBrains' 47% (fully agent-generated). If you review agent output daily, has review time per PR changed for you?
