## First comment by the submitter

> Primary sources, since the post is mostly a reading of them:
>
> - Atlas study (https://stripe.com/blog/top-solo-founder-traits): 63% of C corps formed so far in Q2 2026 were solo. Median first-six-month solo revenue fell 23% in 2025 while the top decile rose 19%; the top-to-median ratio went from about 34x four years ago to 61x.
> - Platform survey (https://stripe.com/blog/saas-platform-trends-in-AI-era): 200 SaaS leaders, March 2026. Seats as primary model 16% in 2026, 11% expected in 2027. One in four plan seats plus separate agent billing.
> - SaaS Index (https://www.stripeeconomics.com/p/the-saaspocalypse-was-more-like-a): non-AI SaaS pay-in volume across about 72,000 businesses a week, now above 30% year over year.
> - Solopreneur proxy (https://www.stripeeconomics.com/p/the-age-of-the-solopreneur): more than twice as many solopreneurs above $1M in 2025 as in 2023.
>
> Caveats: everything is Stripe data about Stripe users, the survey numbers are expectations, the top-decile traits are correlations, and nothing here measures margins. For people running small SaaS: did you move off per-seat pricing this year, and what replaced it?
