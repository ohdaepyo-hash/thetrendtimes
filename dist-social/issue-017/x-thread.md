### 1/7

592 solo founders asked The Startup Project for a directory listing between May and October. 80% had launched.

82% reported no revenue. 15 of the 592 reported $1,000 or more a month. All self-reported.

What 6 primary sources say about micro-SaaS money:

### 2/7

Price is the first leak. ChartMogul sorted 1,043 companies by what customers paid when they hit $10k MRR.

Median annualized gross revenue retention:
Under $30/mo: 24.6%
$30 to $299: 31.6%
$300 to $2,999: 70.5%

70% still sell to the same segment 3+ years on.

### 3/7

Growth fades too. Across 700+ private software companies in a second ChartMogul report, median growth endurance was 43%: 65% growth in 2024 became 28% in 2025.

Only 18% held or raised their growth rate. A 25-49% grower had a 1-in-25 shot at reaching 100%+.

### 4/7

One lever small enough to copy: ask for a card.

Predis moved its free trial behind a credit card on Sep 10, 2025. MRR has tripled year over year. Fyxer's free-to-paid conversion went from 5% to 35% after the same gate.

Two company accounts, not a measured effect.

### 5/7

AI features change the margin. Growth Unhinged polled 230+ B2B software and AI firms: median target AI gross margin is about 50%, vs 70-80%+ for SaaS.

Hybrid pricing leads at 37%, up from 25%. Under $5M ARR, flat fees still lead at 37%.

### 6/7

OpenMeter's math on the same survey: a 20% discount costs a quarter of profit at an 80% margin and 40% of profit at 50%.

And 30% of outcome-priced and 20% of usage-priced companies say customers self-police usage because they don't trust what the bill will be.

### 7/7

Exits: Acquire.com's webinar recap puts SaaS at about 3-5x profit. Pricing 20% above fair value drew about 2% serious buyer interest, 20% below about 92%.
