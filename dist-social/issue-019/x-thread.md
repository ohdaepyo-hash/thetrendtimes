### 1/7

The average price companies paid for AI inference fell to $0.69 per million tokens in October, down 34% since July.

The median company's AI spend still grew every month over the same stretch.

Five November 2026 reports on why cheaper tokens raise bills:

### 2/7

CloudZero's price is an effective rate that includes cache hits and routing. Companies spend the savings: every efficiency gain becomes headroom, and usage fills it.

Ramp saw the same in October: price per million tokens down 21% in a month, AI spend up 1.65%.

### 3/7

Cost per task beats cost per token. In Databricks' coding-agent benchmark, GPT-6 Luna and Claude Opus 5.5 both solved 89.1% of tasks.

Luna averaged $0.05 per task. Opus 5.5 averaged $1.62, about 32x more.

Gemini 3.8 Flash took 140 turns per task: $11.97.

### 4/7

Single-provider shops are fading. On Databricks' gateway, enterprises using one model provider fell from about 64% in July to 28% by November 2.

Open-weight models went from 7.3% to 30.9% of token traffic. DeepSeek V4.1 and GLM 5.3 tie for third at 93.1%.

### 5/7

Inside coding tools the picture differs. Jellyfish found only 0.5% of engineers picked an open-weight model in late October.

Its median developer burned 58M tokens a week, about 8x a year ago, while cost per merged PR fell from $1,377 to $685.

### 6/7

Teams track the wrong number. JetBrains: 51% of tech managers track token usage, but 30% call it a top metric. Net time gain: 39% value it, 28% track it.

CloudZero: 3% of finance leaders who allocate and measure AI spend failed to secure funding, vs 19%.
