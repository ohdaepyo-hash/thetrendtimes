## First comment by the submitter

> The post reads five November 2026 reports, each with its own sample:
>
> - Databricks AI Index (https://www.databricks.com/blog/introducing-databricks-ai-index-state-enterprise-ai-tokenomics): gateway data from 10,000+ companies plus a coding-agent benchmark on private PRs with hidden tests. GPT-6 Luna and Claude Opus 5.5 both solved 89.1% of tasks at $0.05 and $1.62 per task. Single-provider enterprises fell from about 64% to 28%; open-weight token share rose from 7.3% to 30.9%.
> - CloudZero (https://www.cloudzero.com/blog/ai-economics-pulse-november-2026/): effective price per million tokens, cache hits included, fell from $1.03 in July to $0.69 in October (32 organizations). Median AI spend in a 340+ company panel grew every month.
> - Ramp (https://ramp.com/data/ai-index-nov-2026): price per million tokens down 21% in a month, October AI spend up 1.65%.
> - Jellyfish (https://jellyfish.co/ai-engineering-trends/): 58M tokens per median developer per week, about 8x a year ago; cost per merged PR fell from $1,377 to $685.
> - JetBrains (https://blog.jetbrains.com/research/2026/11/state-of-developer-ecosystem-2026/): 51% of tech managers track token usage, 30% rate it a top metric.
>
> Caveats: CloudZero's price and spend lines come from different company groups, Databricks prices tokens at public list rates on one company's codebase, and none of the five isolates prompt caching savings. Has anyone here measured cost per completed task across providers on their own traffic?
