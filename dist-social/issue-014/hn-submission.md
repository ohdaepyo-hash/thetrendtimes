## First comment by the submitter

> Primary sources, since the post is mostly a reading of them:
>
> - Anthropic (https://www.anthropic.com/news/donating-agent-skills-to-the-agentic-ai-foundation): donated Agent Skills to AAIF on Oct 22. Its own count is 1M skills published and nearly 280M installs across 70+ platforms, with no counting method given.
> - AAIF (https://aaif.io/news/agentic-ai-foundation-announces-four-new-projects): 3 projects in December 2025, 10 now, adding Agent Skills, Open Responses, OpenEnv and OpenSandbox.
> - Spec (https://agentskills.io/specification): two required fields, ~100 tokens of metadata loaded at startup, body under 5,000 tokens loaded on activation.
> - MCP (https://blog.modelcontextprotocol.io/posts/mcp-release-cycle/ and SEP-3398): releases on the last Tuesday of March and September, next 2027-03-30, soft freeze 2.5 months out in regular cycles.
> - AWS (https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/govern-and-discover-records-with-common-metadata-in-aws-agent-registry/): metadata schemas and approval gates for agent, MCP server and skill records.
>
> Caveats: skills have no versioned release or packaging standard yet, and the March 2027 freeze dates come from a separate transition calendar. If you maintain an MCP server: does a fixed six-month cadence change how you plan SDK upgrades?
