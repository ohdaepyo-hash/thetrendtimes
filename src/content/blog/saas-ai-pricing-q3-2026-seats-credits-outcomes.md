monday.com put a dollar figure on its AI business this quarter: $36 million in AI annual recurring revenue (ARR), counting AI credits, agents and vibe ([monday.com](https://ir.monday.com/news-and-events/news-releases/news-details/2026/monday-com-Announces-Third-Quarter-2026-Results-new/default.aspx)). Quarterly revenue reached $377.5 million, up 19% year over year. Klaviyo grew faster, at 25%, and printed no AI revenue line at all ([Klaviyo](https://investors.klaviyo.com/newsroom/news/news-details/2026/Klaviyo-Delivers-Solid-Q3-with-Enterprise-Growth-Multi-Product-Strength-and-Agent-Traction/default.aspx)).

If you run pricing at a B2B software company, someone has probably asked which of these signals to copy. Should AI ride on seats, sell as credits, or charge per outcome? Earnings releases show revenue and retention, rarely the price book.

So this comparison sets four Q3 2026 earnings releases (monday.com, Klaviyo, Freshworks and Kaltura) next to a 75-row academic audit of AI pricing pages ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30701v2)). The sections below show where the two agree, where they split and why. The last section turns the overlap into a pricing verdict and checks you can run on your own billing data.

## What Each Q3 2026 Report Actually Measures

All four vendors closed the quarter on 2026-09-30 and reported within one week. Freshworks went first on November 3 ([Freshworks](https://ir.freshworks.com/news/news-details/2026/Freshworks-Reports-Record-Third-Quarter-2026-Results/default.aspx)) and Klaviyo followed on November 4. monday.com and Kaltura both reported on November 9 ([Kaltura](https://investors.kaltura.com/news-releases/news-release-details/kaltura-announces-financial-results-third-quarter-2026)). Timing is not the source of any gap below.

Definitions are. Net revenue retention (NRR) measures how much revenue the existing customer base produced a year later, including expansion and churn. Each company sets its own base and rules for it. Freshworks calls its version net dollar retention and also restates it in constant currency.

Profit figures differ the same way. Every release pairs GAAP results with company-adjusted non-GAAP or adjusted EBITDA (earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortization) figures, and each company chooses its own adjustments. Only monday.com reports a dollar AI ARR figure, and it defines what goes in.

The pricing audit measures something else entirely. Pratik Gupta coded public pricing pages and documentation captured on 2026-09-16 and 2026-09-17, not invoices or contracts ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30701v2)). The dataset has 75 rows: 52 AI-native product rows from the Forbes AI 50 (2026), 14 cloud platform rows and nine rows in a purposive cohort. OpenAI and Anthropic get two rows each, one for the API and one for the consumer app.

The purposive cohort holds distinctive designs chosen on purpose, so the paper keeps it out of every prevalence figure. Gupta works at Stripe, wrote the paper as independent research in a personal capacity, and excluded the employer from the sample.

## Where the Q3 Numbers Agree on Existing-Account Expansion

Past the blended numbers, three of the four break out customer cohorts, and each shows growth concentrating in large accounts. All four describe expansion among existing customers as the main engine.

At monday.com, customers above $50,000 in ARR grew 26% to 5,038 and posted NRR of 114% ([monday.com](https://ir.monday.com/news-and-events/news-releases/news-details/2026/monday-com-Announces-Third-Quarter-2026-Results-new/default.aspx)). Accounts above $100,000 grew 33% to 2,127, and those above $500,000 grew 58% to 123. Paid customers with more than 10 users grew only 5%. The largest single customer runs more than 87,000 seats.

Klaviyo shows the same slope. Customers above $50,000 in ARR rose 33% to 4,725, those above $250,000 rose 49% to 390, and those above $500,000 rose 57% to 105 ([Klaviyo](https://investors.klaviyo.com/newsroom/news/news-details/2026/Klaviyo-Delivers-Solid-Q3-with-Enterprise-Growth-Multi-Product-Strength-and-Agent-Traction/default.aspx)). The full base is more than 211,000 customers. Customers using three or more products account for 22% of ARR.

Freshworks repeats it at a smaller scale. Customers above $100,000 in ARR grew 25% to 1,821, while those above $5,000 grew 5% to 25,498 ([Freshworks](https://ir.freshworks.com/news/news-details/2026/Freshworks-Reports-Record-Third-Quarter-2026-Results/default.aspx)). New multi-product employee experience (EX) deals grew more than 35%, according to CEO Dennis Woodside. The company passed $1 billion in ARR after the quarter closed, ahead of its year-end estimate.

Kaltura's AI story is an expansion story too. Of 15 deals that included AI offerings, 12 were expansions or renewals with existing customers ([Kaltura](https://investors.kaltura.com/news-releases/news-release-details/kaltura-announces-financial-results-third-quarter-2026)). Nine included Agentic Avatars and eight included Content Lab. The quarter also brought 11 six-figure deals and three seven-figure deals.

The second point of agreement is where AI sits: on top of an existing subscription. Two of the four make this explicit. monday.com builds its AI ARR from credits, agents and vibe on a platform it still counts in users and seats. Kaltura sells AI mostly into accounts it already serves.

## Where the Q3 Numbers Split on Retention and AI Disclosure

The blended figures diverge fast. Revenue growth runs from 8% at Kaltura to 25% at Klaviyo, and NRR runs from 97% to 108% ([Kaltura](https://investors.kaltura.com/news-releases/news-release-details/kaltura-announces-financial-results-third-quarter-2026), [Klaviyo](https://investors.klaviyo.com/newsroom/news/news-details/2026/Klaviyo-Delivers-Solid-Q3-with-Enterprise-Growth-Multi-Product-Strength-and-Agent-Traction/default.aspx)). Three causes explain most of the gap, and only one of them is about AI.

### Customer Mix Moves Retention More Than AI

Kaltura's 97% NRR improved for a second straight quarter, yet segment results pull in opposite directions ([Kaltura](https://investors.kaltura.com/news-releases/news-release-details/kaltura-announces-financial-results-third-quarter-2026)). Enterprise, Education and Technology revenue grew 14%. Media and Telecom fell 10%, which Kaltura ties to high churn in 2025.

Freshworks held NRR at 104%, level with Q2 2026 and down from 105% a year earlier ([Freshworks](https://ir.freshworks.com/news/news-details/2026/Freshworks-Reports-Record-Third-Quarter-2026-Results/default.aspx)). That flat line sits next to 25% growth in its largest cohort. A blended NRR hides which customers expand.

### Three Kinds of AI Disclosure

The AI numbers cannot be added up, because each company reports a different unit. monday.com reports $36 million in AI ARR and more than 6 million monday agents interactions in less than two quarters ([monday.com](https://ir.monday.com/news-and-events/news-releases/news-details/2026/monday-com-Announces-Third-Quarter-2026-Results-new/default.aspx)). Kaltura reports deal counts and $3.8 million in combined TCV, up from $1.0 million in Q2 2026 ([Kaltura](https://investors.kaltura.com/news-releases/news-release-details/kaltura-announces-financial-results-third-quarter-2026)).

Klaviyo describes products rather than dollars. The release covers a new Composer version with connectors, skills and artifacts, Klaviyo Headless, and embedded apps for Shopify and Wix ([Klaviyo](https://investors.klaviyo.com/newsroom/news/news-details/2026/Klaviyo-Delivers-Solid-Q3-with-Enterprise-Growth-Multi-Product-Strength-and-Agent-Traction/default.aspx)). Freshworks mentions AI through an IDC MarketScape recognition for AI-enabled IT service management and gives no AI revenue figure.

### Four Margin Definitions

Profit comparisons break on definitions. monday.com's non-GAAP operating margin was 24% against 8% GAAP ([monday.com](https://ir.monday.com/news-and-events/news-releases/news-details/2026/monday-com-Announces-Third-Quarter-2026-Results-new/default.aspx)). Klaviyo posted 15% non-GAAP against a GAAP operating margin of -1% ([Klaviyo](https://investors.klaviyo.com/newsroom/news/news-details/2026/Klaviyo-Delivers-Solid-Q3-with-Enterprise-Growth-Multi-Product-Strength-and-Agent-Traction/default.aspx)).

Freshworks reported a 29.8% non-GAAP operating margin, which Woodside rounded to 30% in the release quote ([Freshworks](https://ir.freshworks.com/news/news-details/2026/Freshworks-Reports-Record-Third-Quarter-2026-Results/default.aspx)). Kaltura leads with a 77% subscription gross margin and $6.0 million in adjusted EBITDA, a different layer of the income statement ([Kaltura](https://investors.kaltura.com/news-releases/news-release-details/kaltura-announces-financial-results-third-quarter-2026)).

AI cost enters here, and only one company names it. monday.com's co-CEOs Roy Mann and Eran Zinman said the AI-first transition introduces near-term operational headwinds ([monday.com](https://ir.monday.com/news-and-events/news-releases/news-details/2026/monday-com-Announces-Third-Quarter-2026-Results-new/default.aspx)). Its Q4 guidance of $380 million to $382 million implies 14% growth, against 20% for full-year 2026.

## Why the Pricing Audit Explains the Split

The audit shows the price structure the earnings releases leave out. The table lists its main figures with cohort and denominator. All come from pages captured 2026-09-16 to 2026-09-17 ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30701v2)).

| Finding | Count | Cohort | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Subscription with usage quota (P6) | 16 of 29 observable rows (55%); 45% to 61% under hybrid boundary tests, 41% to 65% with product-line recoding | AI-native | [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30701v2) |
| No public pricing | 23 of 52 rows (44%); 12 of those 23 route to a demo or sales contact | AI-native | [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30701v2) |
| Outcome-based pricing | 1 of 52 rows (Sierra, no public rate card) | AI-native | [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30701v2) |
| Consumption-based pricing | 12 of 14 rows | Cloud platforms | [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30701v2) |
| Outcome-based pricing | Agentforce, Fin and Zendesk AI, all in customer support | Purposive, excluded from prevalence | [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30701v2) |

### Credits on Top of Seats Explain monday.com's AI Line

Seat-based vendors absorb AI without repricing the seat, the paper finds ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30701v2)). GitHub Copilot adds consumptive premium requests on top of seats. Microsoft bundles Copilot into Microsoft 365 seats, on plans of $18 to $32 per user per month in the September 2026 capture. Notion adds credit-based AI on top of its seat plans.

That structure creates a separable unit. A credit has its own meter, so a vendor can total it and call it AI ARR, as monday.com does with $36 million ([monday.com](https://ir.monday.com/news-and-events/news-releases/news-details/2026/monday-com-Announces-Third-Quarter-2026-Results-new/default.aspx)). When AI ships inside existing tiers with no separate meter, there may be no line to report. That is one plausible reason Klaviyo and Freshworks describe AI in product terms.

### Sales-Led Pricing Explains Kaltura's Deal Counts

The audit's undisclosed rows point to sales-led pricing. Of 23 AI-native rows with no public price, 12 send buyers to a demo or sales contact ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30701v2)). Kaltura's AI disclosure reads like that motion: deal counts, TCV, and six- and seven-figure contracts rather than a usage meter ([Kaltura](https://investors.kaltura.com/news-releases/news-release-details/kaltura-announces-financial-results-third-quarter-2026)).

### Outcome Pricing Stays in Customer Support

Outcome pricing is rare in the data. Only one of 52 AI-native rows lists it, and every outcome-priced example in the purposive cohort sits in customer support ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30701v2)). Intercom's Fin charges $0.99 per resolution with a monthly minimum of 50 resolutions. Salesforce's Agentforce charges per conversation. The paper found no outcome-based example outside customer support.

Support is the category where an outcome can be counted from product logs: a ticket closes or it does not. Freshworks sells service software, yet its Q3 release reports no outcome-priced metric ([Freshworks](https://ir.freshworks.com/news/news-details/2026/Freshworks-Reports-Record-Third-Quarter-2026-Results/default.aspx)). The earnings data and the audit agree that outcome pricing has not reached the revenue line in a way vendors disclose.

## What Q3 Earnings and Pricing Audits Do Not Measure

The earnings side has hard limits. Four companies are a convenience set, not a sample of SaaS. Non-GAAP margins and AI ARR are company-defined, and none of the four releases publishes a price book or per-unit AI price ([monday.com](https://ir.monday.com/news-and-events/news-releases/news-details/2026/monday-com-Announces-Third-Quarter-2026-Results-new/default.aspx)).

The audit has its own. It codes what pricing pages say, not what customers sign or pay ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30701v2)). Gupta designed the taxonomy and did the primary coding. An independent coder recoded a random 20% (15 of 75 rows) and matched on 8 of 11 observable rows (72.7%), with Cohen's kappa of 0.52 on observable rows and 0.71 when observability is included. The paper calls this a consistency check, not a reliability estimate for the population.

Trend claims are off the table. A fixed panel of 39 companies had observable pages at n=9, 14, 20 and 27 across four checkpoints, so the mix changes between them ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30701v2)). Among seven companies seen at all four points, one changed composition: Gamma moved from flat seat pricing in mid-2023 to subscription with usage quota in mid-2024. The dataset itself awaits release.

The counter-case deserves a hearing. Outcome pricing may be spreading in enterprise contracts that never reach a pricing page, and 23 of 52 AI-native rows publish no price at all. If that is true, both data sources undercount it in the same direction.

## Pricing Verdict for B2B SaaS Teams

The verdict: keep the subscription as the anchor and sell AI as a metered quota inside it, the model in 55% of observable AI-native rows in the audit ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30701v2)). Report AI revenue only if your billing system meters AI as its own unit. Hold outcome pricing for work your product can attribute, which today means support-style resolutions.

Watch large accounts, not the blended rate. All four vendors grew fastest in their biggest cohorts, and monday.com's NRR reached 114% above $50,000 in ARR against 108% overall ([monday.com](https://ir.monday.com/news-and-events/news-releases/news-details/2026/monday-com-Announces-Third-Quarter-2026-Results-new/default.aspx)).

### Decide This Quarter

- Export 90 days of AI credit consumption per account from your billing system, and flag the top decile by usage against seat count.
- Add a monthly credit quota to one existing plan tier, and run it on a single customer segment for a full quarter before changing list prices.
- Split NRR by ARR cohort in your finance dashboard, starting with accounts above $50,000, and stop reporting only the blended figure.

### Recheck Next Quarter

- Tag every invoice line in your billing system as seat, credit, usage or outcome, so an AI ARR figure can be totalled if you choose to disclose one.
- Price a resolution or other outcome only after your product logs can attribute ticket closure to the AI agent, and test it against a per-conversation rate.
- Download the 75-row dataset when Gupta releases it, and map your own price book to its primitives to see which pattern you match.
